# PPL-01: local bowl character review, 2026-11-11

Status: prototype browser evidence, not a finished crowd, fit approval, or device acceptance. The two local characters are the approved-source Rocketbox review rigs with the prototype Kenya/Spain color variants and retargeted Idle_Loop and Sitting_Idle_Loop clips. Their GLBs are not committed or included in the production build.

To reproduce: export the animated review GLBs with `blender/people/prototype_web.py`, place `Female_Adult_01.glb` and `Male_Adult_01.glb` in ignored `app/.local-people-review/`, run `npm run dev --prefix app`, open `/?people-review=1`, select People review and then Stand or Sit. The dev server serves only those named local files; a production build does not serve or package them. `?match-review=1` passes the separate prototype-players option to football; that feature requires its own local match files and review.

The review uses the actual lower-tier seat instances 635 and 638 in the current perimeter GLB. A downward ray intersects each modeled pan at y=8.5183 m, and the lower-tier tread in front at y=8.0734 m. Sitting places the pelvis joint 0.120 m above the pan; this is a placement reference, not a measurement of body-to-seat contact. For four samples at 0, 0.5, 1 and 1.5 seconds, the lowest skinned mesh vertex was 0.0070 m above the tread for the first figure and 0.0624 m above for the second. The second has a visible foot clearance requiring rig/contact work. Standing at the same sampled times, lowest-vertex offsets ranged from -0.0014 to 0 m for the first and -0.0022 to 0 m for the second. Lowest vertices are only a proxy for shoe-to-tread clearance and do not establish both feet contact, absence of clipping or buttock-to-seat contact. Numeric values are model coordinates, not physical venue measurements.

Chrome headless on this host showed both standing and seated characters in the bowl. No prototype GLB was requested before selecting People review; the two local GLBs loaded on selection; switching back to Venue removed the review instance. Browser console had no errors in this run. The review uses temporary neutral light to make unbaked figures visible. Stills and sample JSON are in `%TEMP%/talanta-demo-qa-20261109/`; they are not public artifacts. The build passed with 111 modules, and the prototype GLBs were absent from `dist`; the mode-controller suite passed 3/3.

## Foot support follow-up — 2026-11-11

The local review now applies a two-segment leg correction after each animation update. It holds the pelvis placement fixed, preserves upper- and lower-leg lengths, preserves ankle orientation, and raycasts the lower-tier tread below each shoe. If the leg is nearly straight, the ankle may move horizontally toward the hip to reach the tread. An unreachable target is clamped. For measurement, a shoe's sample consists of vertices with at least 50% combined weight on its foot and toe bones; its lowest world-space vertex is compared to the modeled tread beneath that point. This is not a complete shoe-sole contact or collision test.

In headless Chrome, 21 evenly spaced samples across each full sitting and standing loop were checked for both left and right shoes of both rigs (168 shoe samples). All sampled lowest-weighted-vertex offsets were within 0.001 mm of the tread; the seated pelvis references remained 0.120 m above the modeled seat pan. The sample record is `people_bowl_foot_samples_2026-11-11.json`. The seated preview was visually inspected and the figures appear supported by their seats, but buttock-to-pan contact and absence of all mesh intersections are not yet geometrically verified. The shoe samples measure model geometry rather than real-world fit. The extra per-frame skin sampling and raycasts have not been benchmarked on the named laptop or phone. This remains a local, opt-in review; no character assets were added to a production build.

Software checks: 6 focused tests passed (leg reach, segment lengths, unreachable targets, and mode lifecycle). No browser errors were observed in the sample run.
