# Handoff: twin v0.2 (parked for review)

Date: 2026-10-17
Branch: claude/hopeful-archimedes-lo15is
Run: `cd app && npm install && npm run dev`. Test mode on a machine without a GPU: add `?lite` to the URL. Turn off post-processing: `?nopost`.

## What is in the build

- Fully built stadium as the default view. Construction replay is a separate mode (Sentinel-2 roof timeline).
- Bowl v2 from the historians video (JWNowpO44rw): white shell, louvres behind a diamond lattice, eight shields with crossed spears, membrane roof with ribs, dark plinth, green lower tier, red upper tiers, glass VIP band.
- 56,972 individual seats, from the estimated tiers. The official figure is 60,000, so the model is 5.05% short. This fails check V4 (2% limit). The capacity gate stays failed until the bowl dimensions and row structure are resolved. Do not change the seat spacing to force a match.
- Roof opening sized to Sentinel-2 (about 9,900 m2). The earlier version used the OSM ring (16,700 m2), which was wrong.
- North forecourt: an eye-shaped plaza, a fountain pool with animated jets (colour at night), light masts, and about 300 people for scale (Visualization).
- Focus chips with fly-to: Overview, Main forecourt, Fountain, Shield facade, Ngong Hills, bowl, pitch features, roof, seating, training pitches, track, site. A Tour button cycles through them.
- Ambient occlusion and bloom (WebGPU/WebGL2 render pipeline). Not yet seen on a real GPU.
- Ngong Hills on the skyline, from GLO-30 terrain to 20 km. Lamwia is 2,441 m.

## Known issues (fix next)

1. Fixed 2026-10-17: the shields have a low emissive floor (0.22) and a brighter red, and the spears sit behind the shield. Check on a real GPU.
2. From a distance the facade reads grey, not white. The louvre texture and the lattice shading need tuning on a real GPU.
3. The ground texture is 10 m Sentinel-2. It looks blurry close up. Plan: bake a 1.5 m stylised ground (land classes, noise, hillshade, crisp roads, contact shadows).
4. Partly fixed 2026-10-17: the 2,500 trees nearest the bowl have a trunk and two crowns; the rest are single crowns. Species variety is roadmap P3.
5. The positions of the plaza, the fountain and the shields are estimates from frames. The training pitch and track polygons come from OSM and look flat.
6. Night city: no window lights, street lights or traffic yet. The jeantimex Tokyo reference shows the target.
7. Post-processing is untested on a GPU. If it fails at set-up or during a render, the app falls back to a plain render and logs a warning.
8. The roof membrane is now opaque (was 97% transparent): no sorting artifacts, and it shades better. The roof layer slider still fades it.
