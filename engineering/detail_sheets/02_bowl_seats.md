## Conflicts

- Roof underside: Render 03 shows a smooth, rounded inner canopy edge with repeated arch-like ribs. The real frames show a pronounced diagonal steel grid and crossing members beneath the canopy.
- Opening-edge lights: Render 03 shows a continuous string of small lights. The inspected roof frames show discrete floodlight heads and do not confirm that light string.
- Goal nets: Render 03 shows nets. They are not clear in the inspected real frames.
- Pitch boards: Render 03 shows a dark continuous perimeter board. The frames show a low pitch edge, but do not confirm a continuous board.
- Seat row counts: The render gives clearer tier bands than the frames. It does not establish the real row count, so the row counts above are provisional.
- The ledgers seat capacity value of 56,972 is a software model count. It does not match the published 60,000 capacity value in the project plan.
