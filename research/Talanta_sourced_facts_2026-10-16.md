## Site location (checked against data)

- The stadium is at Jamhuri Grounds / Jamhuri Park, off Ngong Road (Citizen Digital and Eastleigh Voice on the 1 March 2024 groundbreaking; People Daily).
- Satellite and OSM data put the bowl centre at 36.74965 E, 1.30602 S. See `gis/coverage_report.md`.
- No source links the site to the Kenya Railways golf course.

## Documents to request (they are not online)

1. NEMA EIA licence and study report for "Talanta Sports City / Jamhuri Grounds". Ask NEMA HQ or use an Access to Information Act request.
2. The Gazette notice of June 2026 that declares the protected area. It may give the plot number and a boundary.
3. The Auditor-General's special audit of the contract, through the Public Accounts Committee papers.
4. The National Assembly Sports and Culture Committee report on the 1 Oct 2026 inspection.
5. The Linzi FinCo 003 Trust information memorandum (NSE listing). It may describe the project.
6. KURA's ESIA for the Talanta-Bomas road network (submitted 5 Aug 2026). It is available on paper at NEMA HQ and may hold a site plan of the surroundings.

## Contradictions

- Built-up area 94,150 vs 98,150 m2.
- VIP boxes 52 vs 53.
- One Sep 2025 article said "retractable roof". The architect later said a fixed canopy. Use the fixed canopy. Sentinel-2 agrees.
- Mini-stadium 1,500 vs 2,000 seats.
