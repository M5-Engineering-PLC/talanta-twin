The bake uses the staged Godot GLB. crowd.json records its SHA-256 hash.
animation_phone.bin and crowd_phone.json are the phone set: LOD1 only, 12 frames per clip.
MeshoptSimplifier from three.js reduces the mesh. Its license is MIT.
Clap, cheer, and wave use hand-keyed bone rotations over Quaternius sitting clips.
The other slots use the supplied Quaternius animation clips.
CMU motion capture data is not used.

Crowds are Visualization. They are not validated safety, capacity, or crowd-flow evidence.
Rendered fan counts do not show venue capacity.
