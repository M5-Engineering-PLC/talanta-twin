Pilot discussion brief

Start with a shared view.

A proposed first commission for Talanta Stadium.

Initial deliverable
A browser-based stadium experience with day and night scenes, weather controls, and one scripted football scenario.
Inputs to agree
Authorized plans, site access, source permissions, stakeholder priorities, hosting, and data ownership.
Acceptance criteria
Review geometry against approved sources, test on agreed devices, and obtain stakeholder sign-off on the scene and claim labels.
Commercial scope
Timeline, price, support, and ownership terms follow an agreed scope. No quote or delivery date is implied.
