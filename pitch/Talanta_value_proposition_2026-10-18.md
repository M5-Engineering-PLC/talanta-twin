# Talanta Twin value proposition

Talanta Twin gives Kenya a digital front door to Talanta Stadium. Visitors can explore before they arrive, while event organizers and sponsors can preview what the venue offers. Built by Kenyan creative and technical talent, the proposed platform brings stadium tours, football, concerts, and national celebrations into one accessible browser experience. A focused pilot will demonstrate its value for venue promotion, event enquiries, and public access to approved information. The venue operator’s needs will guide further development.
